The visualizer effects (`rvp-viz`) and the library views are our own code. `../unicorn-viz` (MIT) was read for the look (neon bars with peak caps, a beat-driven starfield, plasma, tunnel) and for the idea of driving effects from FFT bands and beat tracking; The three scenes added on 2026-10-06 (Bass machine, Unicorn Tears, Disco ball; `crates/rvp-viz/src/effects/scenes.rs`) take their ideas and look from the Unicorn Viz effects of the same spirit (MIT, same owner); they are CPU drawing written from scratch for this player, and no code, shader or asset from Unicorn Viz is used, so no notice is carried. The embedded fonts (Space Grotesk, JetBrains Mono, Noto Sans; SIL OFL 1.1) are unchanged.

Dev/test only (not shipped): `libfuzzer-sys` 0.4 and `cargo-fuzz` (MIT OR Apache-2.0 OR NCSA; `fuzz/`, never part of a build of the player), `@playwright/test` 1.63 (Apache-2.0, `tests/e2e`, with a Chromium build it downloads), `matroska-demuxer` (Zlib OR MIT OR Apache-2.0), `mp4` (MIT),
`ffmpeg`/`ffprobe` (with its libx264 encoder) as external binary oracles and fixture makers (never linked, never distributed, no source read).
`vp9dec` 0.1.1 (MIT) was the other M7 VP9 candidate: bit-exact too, but 3 to 4 times slower, so not used (kept as an oracle option). `rusty_h264-decoder` (BSD-2-Clause) and `h264-reader` (MIT/Apache-2.0) were considered as extra oracles and are not used.
